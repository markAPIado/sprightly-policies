Sprightly Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Sprightly for Android

In short: Sprightly collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing over the internet. Everything it saves stays on your phone.

Sprightly is an interval-walking timer (3 minutes fast, 3 minutes slow) made by Mark Apiado, the developer. This policy explains what Sprightly stores, what it doesn't do, and why it asks for the permissions it uses.

What Sprightly stores on your phone

To show your history and remember your choices, Sprightly saves the following only on your device:

  • Your walks: when each walk started, which workout you chose, how long you walked, your fast-walking minutes, and whether you finished it.
  • Your settings: your chosen workout, your Custom workout, warm-up and cool-down, and which cues (voice, chime, vibration, countdown) are on.
  • The walk in progress, so it can continue if the app is closed during a walk.
  • Two small flags: that you've seen the health note, and that Sprightly has asked for notification permission.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What Sprightly doesn't do

  • It doesn't create accounts or ask for your name, email or any contact details.
  • It doesn't send data over the internet, and it contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • It doesn't access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.
  • It doesn't read health or fitness data from other apps, count your steps, or read your phone's sensors.
  • It doesn't sell or share any data, because it has none to share.

Spoken cues

Voice cues (such as "Fast walking, 3 minutes" or "3, 2, 1") are spoken by your phone's own text-to-speech service. Sprightly passes it only these short phrases, never personal information.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's backup for your phone, Android may include Sprightly's saved walks and settings in your device backup to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. This is managed by Android and your Google account settings, not by Sprightly. Sprightly itself sends nothing.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your phone, you're in control: uninstalling Sprightly, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of it. The developer holds no copy.

Children

Sprightly is a fitness app for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Health

Sprightly is a walking timer, not a medical device, and doesn't give medical advice. If you have a health condition, check with a healthcare professional before starting a new exercise routine.

Changes to this policy

If Sprightly changes how it handles data, this page will be updated with a new effective date before the change reaches the app.
